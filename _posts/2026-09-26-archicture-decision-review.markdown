Checking how your organisation is actually making decisions is generally humbling. 
I had good outcomes out of running this as a 90 min workshop



![decision review overview]({{site.images}}/2026/2026-decision-review.png)

## Who to invite
I recommend to include the technical leadership of your organisation. Generally the CTO, CPO, Head of Engineering , Head of Data, Head of Product, Tech leads, etc. 

## Format

The goal behind this review is two-fold to learn how current decisions are happening and understand the unmet needs. It's better to refrain from solving the issues and making suggestions. It will give people time to consider what they thought and heard from others during this worksop.

After a check-in, start with questions that will help people introspect on the decisions that have happened.
### Part 1

Setting:  Think about the last month and how decisions were made.
Duration: 15 min

Individually consider the questions below. Respond on sticky notes. One idea per sticky note. Stop with the timmer

* Which roles do currently make which decisions?(e.g: Feature flags across teams - CTO only) - 5 min alone
* Consider who has decision rights, How have we made decisions over the last month?(Consensus/Centralised/Describe in your own words) - 5 min alone

After these two questions - discuss for  a few minutes what you see on the board. 

### Part 2

Duration: 40 min

In a new frame and run a [4Ls retrospective](https://www.funretrospectives.com/the-4-ls-liked-learned-lacked-longed-for/) on Significant decisions over the last month

![4Ls retro]({{site.images}}/2026/4Ls.png)

### Part 3

Duration: 20min

Individually consider the questions below. Respond on sticky notes. One idea per sticky note. Stop with the timmer

* What do you(or someone in your role) need from a decision? - 5min alone
* What do other roles (not your role) need from a decision? Respond with a particular role in mind. E.g. Head of  Security needs decisions to consider PII. Product need decisions to consider features affected - 5min alone

Discuss the responses with all the participants. 
Set up a time to reconvene in a few days. In that subsequent meeting changes to the decision making process should be agreed. Mention that the changes can be run as a trial
