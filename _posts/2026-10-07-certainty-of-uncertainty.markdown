### Abstract

Solving problems through systems requires knowledge, creativity, and hard work. Yet, the cold truth of reality often derails our best plans: current technical constraints limits valuable options and collide with shifting expectations. Communication gaps waste irrecoverable time, and data we relied on proves incorrect. How do we break free from the paralysis of uncertainty to navigate this?

This talk is a tour of real-world situations, examining the mindset needed to move forward when your signals tell you the system shouldn't be in this state, and yet here we are. How to learn to understand the system of software and people, find the leverage points, even when the path ahead is unclear.
