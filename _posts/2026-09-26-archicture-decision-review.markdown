Checking how your organisation is actually making decisions is generally humbling. 
I had good outcomes running this as a 90 min workshop


![decision review overview]({{site.images}}/2026/2026-decision-review.png)

## Who to invite
I recommend to include the technical leadership of your organisation. Invite your technical leadership: typically the CTO, CPO, and the Heads of Engineering, Data and Product, plus tech leads. 

## Format

The goal behind this review is two-fold to learn how current decisions are happening and understand the unmet needs. It's better to refrain from solving the issues and making suggestions. Holding back gives people time to reflect what they thought and heard from others during this workshop.

After a check-in, start with questions that will help people introspect on the decisions that have happened.

### Part 1

Setting:  Think about the last month and how decisions were made.
Duration: 15 min

Individually consider the questions below. Respond on sticky notes. One idea per sticky note. Stop with the timer

* Which roles make which decisions?(e.g: Feature flags across teams - CTO only) - 5 min alone
* Consider who has decision rights, How have we made decisions over the last month?(Consensus/Centralised/Describe in your own words) - 5 min alone

After these two questions - discuss for  a few minutes what you see on the board. 

### Part 2

Setting:  Think about the last month and how decisions were made.
Duration: 40 min

Run a 4Ls retrospective(( Learned, Lacked, Loathed, Loved )) on Significant decisions over the last month

![4Ls retro]({{site.images}}/2026/4Ls.png)

### Part 3

Setting:  Think about the last quarter and how decisions were made.
Duration: 20 min

Individually consider the questions below. Respond on sticky notes. One idea per sticky note. Stop with the timer 

* What do you (or others in your role) need from a decision? - 5 min alone
* What do other roles (not your role) need from a decision? Respond with a particular role in mind. E.g. Head of  Security needs decisions to consider PII. Product need decisions to consider features affected - 5 min alone

Discuss the responses with all the participants. 

Set up a time to reconvene in a few days. In that subsequent meeting changes to the decision making process should be agreed. Mention that the changes can be run as a trial
